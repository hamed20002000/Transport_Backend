# Cargo photos

The picture a load gets when it has no photo of its own (`CargoImageService.cargoPhoto`): the cargo itself when its
type has a photo here, else its vehicle (reefer, tanker, tipper, flatbed, boxtruck), else a truck (truck.jpg). A cargo
with no photo of its own gets a vehicle, never a near one (no apples for dates).
